## Engineering Philosophy

The two main camps I've observed in RF-sensing world are the model-heavy, classical statistical signal processing folks and the data-heavy deep learning folks. In my research, I believe there exists a happy-medium where we can leverage the inductive biases of domain expertise while not sacrificing the functional expressivity of deep learning.
